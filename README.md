# Folio Writing Studio

A free writing program for short stories, novellas and novels. It runs in your web browser, works
offline, and can be installed like an ordinary app.

## Using Folio

1. Open the Folio link you were sent, in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
2. Click **Install app**. (On iPhone or iPad, use Safari's Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.)
3. Take the two-minute tour, or press **F1** at any time for the full guide.

## Your writing is private

There is no account and no server. Your projects are saved only on your own device, and nothing you
write is ever uploaded. To keep a copy outside the browser, choose **More › Save to a file on disk**
and put the file in Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud or Google Drive.

## Updates

When Folio is improved, you'll see "A new version of Folio is ready". Click **Update now**. Your
writing isn't affected.
